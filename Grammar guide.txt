<main>
  <h2>Basic Grammar Rules</h2>

  <section class="card">
    <h3>1. Nouns</h3>
    <p>A noun names a person, place, animal, or thing.</p>
    <p><strong>Example:</strong> The student reads a book.</p>
  </section>

  <section class="card">
    <h3>2. Pronouns</h3>
    <p>Pronouns replace nouns.</p>
    <p><strong>Example:</strong> She is a student.</p>
  </section>

  <section class="card">
    <h3>3. Verbs</h3>
    <p>Verbs express actions or states.</p>
    <p><strong>Example:</strong> They study every day.</p>
  </section>

  <section class="card">
    <h3>4. Punctuation</h3>
    <p>A full stop ends a statement.</p>
    <p><strong>Example:</strong> I like reading.</p>
    <p>A question mark ends a question.</p>
    <p><strong>Example:</strong> What is your name?</p>
  </section>

  <section class="card">
    <h3>5. Capital Letters</h3>
    <p>Start sentences and proper names with capital letters.</p>
    <p><strong>Correct:</strong> My name is Ravi.</p>
  </section>
